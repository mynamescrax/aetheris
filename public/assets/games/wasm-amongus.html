iPad may not run this game

Among Us Web downloads ~860 MB and needs over 1 GB of memory. iPads kill tabs past their limit, which is why the page loads, runs a few seconds, then refreshes. The game now loads its big content file on demand instead of all at once, which may be enough on newer iPads — older ones still may not make it.

Newer (M-series) iPads have higher limits and the best chance. You can try, but expect possible crashes.
