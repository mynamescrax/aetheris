Welcome to Cozy Idle Fisher

Catch fish by clicking in the lake while fish are inside your net/crosshair. Press the sell button (keybind 1) to sell the fish in your bucket. Use the money you earn to upgrade stuff in the Shop (keybind S).

The shop lets you buy upgrades and abilities such as:

  • Rod and bait increases fish weight/value
  • Abilities offer fun gameplay such as Wide Net increasing your hitbox for catching fish
  • Fishers, fishboats, and giant fishboats will fish for you automatically
  • Hire traders who will automatically sell fish in your bucket

Keybinds:

  • Abilities: 1–6
  • Shop: S • Travel: T • Bucket: B • Achievements: A • Fishpedia: F • Lootbox: L

Tips:

  • Clicking on a worker will disable them
  • You can move workers around to place them strategically
  • Using two (or more) abilities at the same time can be very powerful!

You can always revisit this later by clicking the Help button.

Happy fishing!
